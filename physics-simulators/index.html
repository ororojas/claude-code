physics-simulators

A 3D physics sandbox and a 4D puzzle game

Both run in the browser on physics engines written for this folder. There is nothing to build: open one and play.

Proving Ground

3D rigid-body simulator

Twelve scenarios on a real-physics engine: dominoes, Jenga, Newton's cradle, a bowling lane with an oil pattern, a wrecking ball and a castle siege. Objects use real materials at their real densities, and you can rerun anything on the Moon, Mars or Jupiter.

Open the simulator →

Ana & Kata

4D puzzle game

You are a hypersphere, and you see the 3D slice of a 4D world. Move ana or kata, the two ways along the fourth direction, to slip past walls that only exist over part of it. Ten levels, plus a lab where you drop tesseracts and 24‑cells and cut them at any angle.

Play the game →

Both pages load Three.js and their fonts from public CDNs, so they need a network connection. They also work when opened straight from disk.

The engines are checked against textbook results:

node --test physics-simulators/tests/*.test.js
