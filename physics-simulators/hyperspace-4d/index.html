ana & kata

A puzzle game in four dimensions. You are a hypersphere, and you see the 3D slice of the world that passes through you.

One more direction. A 4D world has a fourth direction at right angles to the three you know. Its two ways are called ana and kata.

You see a slice. Move ana or kata and the slice changes. A wall that only spans a short stretch of w fades out, and something else fades in.

Ghosts. Things just ana of you glow orange, things just kata of you glow blue. The gauge on the right shows where everything sits in w.

Levels

4d lab

Drop 4D shapes onto a 4D floor and watch their 3D cross-sections. Click a shape to kick it through all four directions. Turn the slice with the sliders to cut the same shapes at a new angle.

shapes

A tesseract's slice can be a cube, a stretched box, a triangular prism or a hexagonal solid, depending on how it is turned. Every one is a true cross-section of the same 4D object.
