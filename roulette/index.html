Menu

Blackjack: the iPad is the dealer. Bet on your seat and tap DEAL, then deal two real cards to each player and play your hands. Tap Double or Split on your seat if you use them. Tap DEALER PLAYS, and once the dealer finishes, everyone taps Win, Push, Lose or Blackjack on their own seat, then PAY OUT.

Tap a spot on the table to bet with the selected player's chips. Tap near a line to split, on a corner for four numbers, and on the outer edge for a street. Swipe across the wheel to launch the ball yourself.

Tip: in Safari, use Share → Add to Home Screen to play full screen.
